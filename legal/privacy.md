## Who we are

YourReset ("we", "us", "the app") is published by **[YOUR LEGAL NAME / BUSINESS NAME]**.
Contact: **[YOUR EMAIL]**.

YourReset is a women's health and fitness companion that adapts workouts and
nutrition guidance to the user's menstrual cycle phase. We take your privacy
seriously, especially given the sensitive nature of cycle and body data.

## Summary

- **All your personal data stays on your device.** We do not operate servers
  that store your cycle, workout, nutrition, or body measurement data.
- We do not sell your data. Ever.
- We do not run advertising in the app.
- The app does not require an account to use.
- You can export or permanently delete all your data from inside the app at
  any time, with one tap.

## Data we collect

YourReset stores the following data **locally on your device only**, using
the operating system's secure preferences store (UserDefaults on iOS):

| Category | Examples | Where it's stored |
|---|---|---|
| Profile | Name, gender, age, weight, height, goal, activity level | Device (UserDefaults / Preferences) |
| Cycle | Cycle start date, cycle length, hormonal contraception status, OPK confirmation | Device |
| Workouts | Completed sets, reps, weight, RPE, exercise substitutions | Device |
| Nutrition | Logged meals and water intake | Device |
| Daily check-ins | Energy, sleep, soreness, cramps, mood | Device |
| Gut tracking | Daily symptom logs, trigger tags, notes | Device |
| Friends leaderboard | Friend names and manually-entered step counts | Device |
| Health (optional) | Step count, active calories, resting heart rate, exercise minutes — read from Apple Health if you grant permission | Device; never sent off-device by YourReset |

We **do not** collect or transmit:
- Email addresses or phone numbers
- Location data
- Device identifiers for tracking
- Crash reports or analytics (as of the current release)

## How we use this data

The data is used only to power the features of the app:
- Cycle phase predictions inform workout intensity and nutrition tips.
- Body metrics compute your estimated daily calorie target (BMR / TDEE).
- Workout history powers progressive-overload suggestions.
- Daily check-ins adjust the day's recommended training volume.

No data leaves your device for these purposes.

## Apple HealthKit (if permission granted)

If you grant permission, YourReset reads the following from Apple Health:
step count, active energy burned, resting heart rate, exercise minutes,
and (optionally) body mass.

We comply with Apple's HealthKit guidelines:
- Health data is used only for displaying your metrics and personalising
  recommendations inside the app.
- Health data is **never** sent to any server.
- Health data is **never** shared with third parties.
- Health data is **never** used for advertising or marketing.
- You can revoke HealthKit access at any time in iOS Settings → Health.

## Subscriptions and payments (if applicable)

If a paid subscription is offered, payment is processed by Apple via the
App Store. We never see your payment information. Subscription receipts are
validated through the App Store. Subscription status may be cached locally
on your device.

To manage or cancel your subscription, use **Settings → Manage Subscription**
in the app or visit Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your iPhone.

## Third-party services

The current release of YourReset uses the following third-party services:
- **Apple App Store** — for app distribution and payment processing.
- **Apple HealthKit** — for reading health data, only with your permission.
- **Capacitor (Ionic)** — the open-source framework the app is built on. No
  Capacitor service receives your data.

If we add analytics, error reporting, or other third-party SDKs in a future
release, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy labels.

## Your rights

You can, at any time, from within the app:
- **Export your data** as a JSON file (Settings → Export data).
- **Permanently delete your account and all data** (Settings → Delete account).
  This wipes every piece of data the app has stored on your device. It is
  irreversible.
- Revoke Apple HealthKit permissions in iOS Settings → Health.

If you are in a jurisdiction with specific data protection laws (GDPR, CCPA,
UK GDPR, etc.), the local-only data model means you have full control: there
is nothing on a server for us to delete on your behalf.

## Children

YourReset is not directed to children under 13. If you are a parent and
believe a child under 13 has used the app, please contact us so we can help.
